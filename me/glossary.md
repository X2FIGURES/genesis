## Origin
<!-- path: trading -->

An origin is a low (uptrend) or a high (downtrend) launched by 2 or more consecutive trend candles, retested twice, and held with no [[Hard close]]. The 15m origin controls the zone. See [[Trigger]].

## Hard close
<!-- path: trading -->

A hard close is a candle that opens and closes beyond the level, against the trend.

## Clean trade
<!-- path: trading -->

A clean trade is a by-the-book trade. The success metric is 10 clean trades, not 10 wins. Optimize the trader first, size later. See [[Trading]].

## EAT
<!-- path: trading -->

Nairobi time, the clock on his charts. Dubai is one hour ahead. See [[Daily rhythm]].
