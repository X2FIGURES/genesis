Near-term skill that pays bills and subscriptions. Market is XAUUSD gold. See [[Ndegwa]], [[Daily rhythm]], and [[Glossary]].

The method is the [[Origin]]. Timeframes are the [[Trigger]]. Size is the [[Lot ladder]]. News and "no trade" live under [[Stand aside]]. Named strategy notes live under [[Strategy notes]].

Start at 0.01 lot (~$10.8 margin). Stop under $4 always. Fixed $20 take-profit until the account grows from $15 to $500, then dynamic. Max 2 trades a session. Two losses and he is out.

The success metric is 10 [[Clean trade]]s, not 10 wins.

## Trigger
<!-- path: trading -->

15m origin controls the zone. 5m is the trending entry zone. A held 1m origin is the only trigger. No clear 1m held origin means no trade. See [[Origin]].

## Lot ladder
<!-- path: trading -->

A win doubles the lot. A loss steps back one rung. 0.01 ($15-35), 0.02 ($35-75), 0.04 ($75-155), 0.08 ($155-315), 0.16 ($315-635).

## Stand aside
<!-- path: trading -->

Stand aside on high-impact news (NFP) until a defined setup forms. No clear 1m held [[Origin]] means no trade.
